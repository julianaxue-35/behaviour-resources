7-day rule Concerning behaviours within the first 7 days are expected during adjustment. If they persist beyond 7 days with no progress, flag for discussion with the behaviour team and behaviour vet.

Fearful body language

Crouched posture, cowering, ears pinned back, wide eyes, tail tucked or low, panting, drooling, trembling, repeated startling, inappropriate urination or defecation, or expressing anal glands.

Inhibited movement

Hesitates, moves slowly or cautiously, or stops moving altogether. Appears shut down but remains aware of surroundings, distinguishing it from catatonia.

Approach/avoidance behaviour

Approaches a person or object then quickly retreats, possibly repeating the cycle. Appears conflicted between curiosity and fear or defensiveness.

Escape behaviour

Panicked flight or attempts to retreat, hide, or reach an exit. May fixate on exit points or jump at windows and doors.

Frenetic movement (concerning)

Extremely active, disorganised, seemingly directionless rapid movement with no obvious purpose.

Catatonia (concerning)

Completely immobile and disconnected from surroundings. Does not respond to obvious stimuli. If touched, stays still as though unable to register the contact.

Defensive aggression (concerning)

Stiffening, whale eye, baring teeth, barking, growling, or snapping. Occurs alongside fearful body language and only when the dog cannot move away. Does not move forward or display offensive body postures.

Offensive aggression (concerning)

Charges forward with ears up, tail stiff and raised, weight forward, upright posture, and prolonged direct hard eye contact. Directed toward people including familiar people.
